# Crisp Pixels (exploration, not live)

The same village, palette and depth pass, drawn with twice the pixel density (late-SNES / GBA detail instead of NES).
Scenes still lay out in 300×210 scene pixels; the canvases are 600×420 and `pixel-hi.js` redraws props on the finer grid.

- Done: ground, water, trees and far trees, houses, her, Rosa, Bea, Mo and Mr. Pip (map), the orchard and bakery close-ups, Dot, apples, baskets, signs.
- Not yet: market and post office close-ups, fishing screen, yard items and gifts, crates of ten, coin and tray icons, hand-drawn chibi bodies.
- Rough cost to finish: 3–5 days of art work, plus a performance check on a real iPad.
- Built on 9698a6d (before Tom's workshop). To ship: rebase on main, convert the remaining art, rerun the village tests and UI audit.
