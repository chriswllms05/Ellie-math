# 02 · Math

Math has one job in this game: **it earns pinballs.** It happens only in **math rounds** at the pinball trees in her camp ([08-camp.md](08-camp.md)). Battles, the pinball machine and the catcher have no math in them. The pay rules and combo are in [00-names-and-numbers.md](00-names-and-numbers.md); this spec turns them into exact rules and covers everything else on the math side. How much a day of math should buy is balanced in [07-economy.md](07-economy.md).

This is all ours: the real game has no math. Starting numbers are marked **(tune)**.

## 1. Who it's for

From the earlier Ellie Math notes (written October 2026, so the placement check in section 3 finds where she is now):

- Third grade, **Singapore Dimensions Math 3**.
- **Behind on addition and subtraction facts to 20**, and still counts many of them on her fingers.
- **Rushes and guesses** when she's unsure.
- Her class words: **partners of 10**, **doubles**, **near doubles**, **make 10**, **subtract from 10**, **subtract the ones first**, **think addition**, **groups of**. Help uses these words and no others.
- Chapter 4 expects times facts for **×0 to ×5 and ×10** from memory.

So: facts to 20 come first and get most of every round until they're caught up; times tables come in as her class reaches them; bigger questions come later.

## 2. Skills

A **fact** is one question with one answer, like 8 + 5. Facts are grouped into **families** that are taught with one strategy. A fact belongs to the **first** family in this list that contains it, so no fact is in two families. Turnarounds are separate facts (3 + 5 and 5 + 3), because she may know one and not the other.

**A family opens** when every family it needs has at least **70%** of its facts fast or solid (tune). Families A1–A4 are open from the start, unless the placement check opens more.

## 3. The placement check (Slice 1)

Her very first math round finds where she is, so she never grinds facts she already knows.

- **How it's framed:** it is simply her first round at her first tree. The tree "wants to see what you already know." No score, no "test", no wrong sound.
- **Pays like any round:** normal pinballs and combo, so it feels like the game.
- **What it asks:** for each family in order (A1 to A11, then B1 and B3 unless Times tables is off), **2 facts** from the middle of its list.
  - Both fast and right: the family **opens**, and all its facts start as **fast** (one fast day credited). They still need a second fast day to become solid, so nothing is assumed for good.
  - One or none: the family stays as it was (open or closed by the normal rules), facts stay **new**.
  - If both facts in two families in a row miss, it skips the families that need those two.
- **No help during placement:** a miss or "I don't know" just says "Okay!" and moves on. Help would make it long and feel like a lesson.
- **"I don't know" is always there**, and is never counted against her.
- **Length:** at most **30 questions or 6 minutes** (tune), whichever is first.
- **If she leaves partway:** what she answered counts; the next round finishes the remaining families. Placement counts as done after 15 answers or every family probed. It never repeats unless the grown-up taps **Redo placement**.

## 4. How a fact grows

### States

| State | Means |
|---|---|
| **new** | Not yet taught. |
| **learning** | Being practised. Counts toward the learning cap. |
| **fast** | Answered fast and right on one day. Needs one more day. |
| **solid** | Answered fast and right on two different days. Hers. |

### Cold and warm answers

The **first** time a fact is asked on a given calendar day (her iPad's local date) is its **cold** answer. Any later ask that day (a repeat after a miss, a second round) is **warm**. **Only cold answers move a fact between states.** Warm answers still pay pinballs and are logged, but they can't make a fact fast or solid: a fact she just saw is easy to remember.

### Transitions (cold answers only)

A fact goes from **new** to **learning** when the round builder first asks it (or placement credits it, section 3). After that:

| From | Fast and right | Slow and right | Wrong, rushed or "I don't know" |
|---|---|---|---|
| learning | → **fast** (fast day 1 = today) | stays learning | stays learning |
| fast | on a later day → **solid** | stays fast | → **learning** (fast day cleared) |
| solid | review level +1 (max 4) | review level → 0 | → **learning** (a **slip**) |

- **Spaced review for solid facts:** next review in **2, 4, 8, 16, 32 days** for review levels 0–4 (tune). A fast cold answer moves it up a level; a slow one sends it back to 0.
- **Fast facts** are due again the next day, to try for the second fast day.
- **A slip** takes nothing away from her in the game. It only puts the fact back into practice; the grown-up view lists it.
- **Learning cap:** at most **8** facts in learning at once (tune). New facts are only introduced while she's under the cap. A turnaround or subtraction partner skips the queue but not the cap.
- **A family is done** when 90% of its facts are solid (tune).

## 5. Building a round

**Length:** 20 questions (tune; grown-up setting 10, 20 or 30). She can do as many rounds a day as she likes. Because fast days are counted per calendar day, five rounds in one day can't make facts solid faster than one.

**Picking (each round):**

1. **Due:** solid facts whose review date has come, and fast facts not yet answered today.
2. **Learning:** every fact in learning.
3. **New:** up to **3 new facts** a round (tune), only while under the cap, from the lowest open family that has new facts (Track A first; Track B within its share).

**Mix (tune):** about half learning, a third due, the rest new. If one group is short, fill from the others; if all are short (the very start), repeat learning facts more often.

**Within a round:**

- Each learning fact appears **up to 3 times**, at least 3 questions apart. Its first ask is cold if it's the first today; the others are warm.
- **After a miss or "I don't know":** the fact comes back **3 to 5 questions later**, once; missed again, once more near the end. Never more than twice.
- **Order:** open with **2 solid facts** (an easy start), never the same fact twice in a row, mix adding and taking away, and **end on a solid fact**.
- **Nothing due and nothing new** (everything she has is solid): the round fills with the solid facts she saw longest ago. It pays the same. The grown-up view notes that it's time to open the next track.
- **Leaving mid-round:** answers so far count, and pinballs already grown stay on the tree ([08-camp.md](08-camp.md)).

## 6. Answering and timing

- **Typed answers only.** Never multiple choice.
- **Number pad:** 3 × 4 grid, keys at least 80 points (iPad layout units) square, acting on touch-down: 1 2 3 / 4 5 6 / 7 8 9 / ⌫ 0 ✓. Up to 3 digits (5 for Track C). ✓ is greyed until she types something.
- **"I don't know"** is its own button, well away from ✓ so it can't be hit by mistake.
- **The clock** starts when the question is fully on screen (after any entry animation; if it's ever read aloud, after the voice ends). It stops when she taps ✓. Time includes any backspacing.
- **Fast:** right, and time ≤ the family's fast time (section 2; the grown-up can change the facts' 3 s anywhere from 2 to 6 s).
- **Slow:** right, and over the fast time. There's no upper limit; after 15 s the "I don't know" button glows gently (tune).
- **Rushed:** wrong, and faster than **1.0 s** (tune). Treated as wrong for pay and the combo, and logged as rushed. After her **second** rushed answer in a round, a one-time friendly note: "Whoa, take a breath. Think, then tap." (tune).
- Taps are ignored for 300 ms after ✓, so a double tap can't answer the next question.

## 7. Pay and combo (Slice 1)

From [00-names-and-numbers.md](00-names-and-numbers.md), as an exact algorithm. A round starts with `streak = 0`, `level = 1`.

| Level | Multiplier | Reached at streak |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | x1 | 0 |
| 2 | x2 | 3 |
| 3 | x3 | 6 |
| 4 | x4 | 10 |

On each answer:

- **Fast and right:** pay `3 × level`, then `streak += 1` and set `level` from the table. (So answers 1–3 pay 3, 4–6 pay 6, 7–10 pay 9, 11 on pay 12.)
- **Slow and right:** pay `1` (never multiplied), then `level = max(1, level − 1)` and `streak` = that level's starting streak (x2 → 3, x1 → 0).
- **Wrong or rushed:** pay `0`, `level = 1`, `streak = 0`.
- **"I don't know":** pay `0`, nothing changes.
- Warm answers pay exactly like cold ones. Placement answers pay normally.

A perfect 20-question round pays **183** pinballs. A typical early round (say 10 fast, 6 slow, 4 misses) pays roughly **35 to 70**, depending on how the fast answers bunch up. These are for [07-economy.md](07-economy.md) to balance against.

**What she sees:**

- The tree she's working at, with a pinball popping onto it for each answer that pays: a big "+6" for fast answers, a small soft "+1" for slow ones ([08-camp.md](08-camp.md) owns the tree).
- A **combo meter** near the question: x1 to x4, filling with each fast answer and glowing hotter at each level. A slow answer cools it one step; a miss lets it fade out.
- A miss has no buzzer and no red X: the right answer and help appear (section 8).
- **End of round:** pinballs grown, best combo, how many fast answers. Then she taps the tree to collect.

## 8. Help (Slice 1)

After a **wrong, rushed or "I don't know"** answer, never after a right one:

1. The question is shown complete, with the right answer (8 + 5 = 13), and a picture of the family's strategy with one line in her class words.
2. A **Got it** button appears after 1.5 s, so she looks first. She doesn't have to type anything.
3. The fact returns 3 to 5 questions later (section 5).

The combo is changed only by the answer itself (section 7), never by the help.

## 9. Honesty, quietly

- **Guessing never pays.** A wrong answer pays nothing and resets the combo; a rushed one also gets the note. "I don't know" is always safe.
- **She can't farm easy facts.** The round builder picks every question.
- **Remembering isn't knowing.** Only cold answers count toward fast and solid, and solid takes two different days.
- **Finger counting** is never mentioned to her. Counting a fact rarely beats 3 seconds, so knowing it pays three to twelve times more than counting it. The grown-up view shows slow-right answers by family, which is where counting shows up.

## 11. What the math engine stores

Fields only; the format and saving are in [11-tech.md](11-tech.md).

**Each fact:** key (e.g. `add:8+5`, `sub:14-8`, `ten:7` for 7 + ☐ = 10, `mul:4x5`, `div:20/5`), family, state, date introduced, fast day 1 (date), date it became solid, review level, next review date, last cold date, last seen time, counts (fast right, slow right, wrong, rushed, "I don't know"), last 5 answer times.

**Each answer:** time stamp, round id, fact key (or generated question and its answer for Track C), family, what she typed, right answer, result (fast, slow, wrong, rushed, don't know), time in ms, cold or warm, help shown, placement or not, combo level before and after, pinballs paid.

**Each round:** id, start and end, tree, number of questions, pinballs grown, best combo, finished or left partway.

**Each family:** open, date opened, done, placement result. **Placement:** families probed, done or not. **Settings:** as in section 10.
